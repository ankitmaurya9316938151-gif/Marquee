Soft Drink

A soft drink is any non-alcoholic beverage that contains natural or artificial sweeteners, flavoring agents, and usually carbonated water. The term "soft" was originally used to distinguish these non-alcoholic beverages from "hard" alcoholic drinks>

Pizza

Pizza is an iconic, globally beloved dish of Italian origin, consisting of a flattened disk of leavened wheat dough. It is traditionally topped with tomato sauce, cheese (like mozzarella), and various other ingredients, and then baked at a very high temperature, historically in a wood-fired oven.

Burger

A burger is a classic sandwich consisting of a cooked patty (typically beef, chicken, or plant-based) placed inside a sliced bun. It is commonly garnished with vegetables like lettuce, tomato, and onion, and topped with condiments such as ketchup, mustard, or mayonnaise.
